01 / MEET FX ORBIT

From subtle depth
to a world of texture.

FX Orbit by Shevtone Audio brings delay, reverb and stereo processing into one creative effects plugin. Add rhythmic echoes, shape the space around an instrument and introduce movement across the stereo field — all from a single interface.

Use each module on its own or switch on all three to build a layered treatment. A central orbital display, a dedicated Motion control and a global Mix knob keep the experience focused on shaping the sound.

INDEPENDENT MODULE ON / OFF
01 — DELAY↗

Make repeats part of the music.

Create echoes that extend a phrase, reinforce a groove or add a rhythmic layer behind the original performance. Time sets the repeat interval, while Feedback controls how much of the delayed signal returns to produce further repeats.

TIME / FEEDBACK / TEMPO SYNC
02 — REVERB↗

Give every sound a place.

Add a sense of depth and atmosphere around your source. Size shapes the perceived scale of the space, while Damping controls the high-frequency character of the reverberation. Move toward a brighter ambience or a softer, darker background.

SIZE / DAMPING
03 — STEREO↗

Bring the stereo field to life.

Shape the spread and movement of your sound. Rate sets the pace of stereo modulation, and Width adjusts the stereo image. Combine the Stereo module with delay and reverb to create a more animated spatial treatment.

RATE / WIDTH / MOTION
02 / A CLOSER LOOK

Every detail.
Within reach.

Dedicated effect sections sit alongside global controls, so you can adjust individual details and the overall treatment without changing views.

01

Time & Feedback

Set the interval between delay repeats and the amount of signal fed back into the delay. Use fewer repeats for a cleaner echo or increase feedback for a denser trail.

DELAY
02

Size & Damping

Adjust the scale and tonal character of the reverb. Size shapes the spatial impression; Damping softens the high-frequency content of the reverberation.

REVERB
03

Rate & Width

Control the speed of stereo modulation and the spread of the stereo image. Rate is displayed in hertz, while Width is displayed as a percentage.

STEREO
04

Motion

A dedicated large control brings spatial movement to the foreground of the workflow. Keep it close while exploring combinations of the three effect modules.

GLOBAL
05

Tempo Sync

Use musical timing for delay-based textures. The interface includes a Tempo Sync control and a note-based Time readout, shown at 1/8 in the screenshot.

TIMING
06

Freeze

A dedicated Freeze switch is available in the global control section for sustained-effect exploration.

PERFORM
07

Mix & Output

Blend the original signal with the processed sound using the global Mix control. Watch the output meters as you balance the overall treatment.

BALANCE
08

Module On / Off

Enable Delay, Reverb and Stereo independently. Build a complete multi-effect treatment or focus on a single module.

WORKFLOW
